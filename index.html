About Us

Anandha Nursery and Primary School is committed to providing quality education in a caring and child-friendly environment. We focus on creating a strong foundation during the early years of a child's education.

Vision

To nurture confident, knowledgeable, responsible and compassionate young learners who are prepared to face the future with confidence.

Mission

Our mission is to provide quality and meaningful education, build a strong academic foundation, encourage curiosity and independent thinking, develop communication and social skills, and promote discipline and good values.
